One trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude is the list’s only potentially missable trophy: guides report the first meditation spot — Lucia on a balcony at home in Chapter 1 — becomes permanently unavailable after that chapter, and there is no mission replay. Do it before Chapter 1 ends, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Sources: GTA Zone, Output Lag.

Roadmap to the platinum

Built from the official descriptions: story chapters and missions first, then side strands and activities, then collectibles and cleanup. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, unlocks when the other 54 are earned.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story

    Chapters 1–4 and the final mission, plus the named missions (Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory). Before Chapter 1 ends: do Lucia’s balcony meditation (Positive Mental Attitude). Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

  2. Stage 2 — Character strands & strangers

    Finish the final mission for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, complete 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, and hunt the unique animal for Bret. These unlock alongside the story, so clear them as they appear.

  3. Stage 3 — Activities & the big counts

    250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range gold medals, 14 Top Fun drone-race wins, 20 race wins, 50 challenges, 5 bank vaults, $250,000 of laundered Marked Bills and $100,000 spent. Try every activity type once for Master of None along the way.

  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles & cleanup

    100 WAiNK locations, 18 meditation spots, 3 collectible card sets, 100 vehicles discovered, every area of Leonida explored, all garage properties bought, 10 vehicles registered to Scooter Bros. Then check the 100% completion list — A Long Road is the last gold before the platinum.
